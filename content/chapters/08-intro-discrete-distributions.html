CHAPTER 8 · LESSON NOTES

Introduction: Discrete Probability Distributions

Starting a new session, building on the basics of probability, random variables, and expected value from before.

The last session covered the basics: random variables, probability distributions, and expected value. This session builds on those ideas with some slightly more advanced ones.

In this session

You will learn about two probability distributions commonly used for discrete random variables:

  • The binomial probability distribution
  • The uniform probability distribution

You will also learn about cumulative probability, which will be useful in the next session on continuous probability distributions.

Prerequisites

This session assumes you already know:

  • The basic rules of probability, specifically the addition rule and the multiplication rule, both covered in Chapter 2, Probability Recap.
  • Some basic combinatorics, in particular the combinations expression, usually written C(n, r) or "n choose r", for the number of ways to choose r items out of n.
